Design notes
SVG gallows uses palette colors: teal scaffold, gold rope, pink for body parts so each wrong guess "lights up" in the danger accent color.
6 wrong guesses allowed: head → body → left arm → right arm → left leg → right leg. No noose — keeping it family-friendly with a stylized art-deco figure.
Word list: Miami neighborhoods, landmarks, slang. Categories shown as chip (Neighborhoods, Food, Culture, etc.) so passengers learn something even if they lose.
Lives shown as dots in header (teal = alive, dim = lost). Max 6 dots.
Accent color for card border-top: #A855F7 (purple — distinct from existing games).
On win: word cells animate gold. On loss: word fills in pink + "The word was BISCAYNE" reveal. Play Again button.
