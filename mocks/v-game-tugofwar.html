3P: each of the 3 columns has its own tap zone. The rope marker moves based on tap-count differential. P3 mashing in the center means both P1 and P2 are pulling against P3 plus each other — last player to reach one end wins.
Design notes
Rope marker position = (P1 taps − P2 taps) / total_target. A lead of ~25% pulls marker near the win zone. At 100% lead — all the way to the edge — game ends immediately.
When time runs out: whoever has the marker on their side wins. If marker is at center = draw (both get a point in a best-of-3 option).
Each tap plays a small haptic pulse (if browser vibration API is available) — critical for feel on tablet.
Tap zones are maximally large (full half-screen) — no button to mis-tap. Touch events not click events so rapid mashing registers every contact.
Accent color for card border-top: #F43F5E (rose-red — distinct from pink #FF5C8A and existing game colors).
3P logic: marker = weighted centroid of all three tap counts. P3 pulling center zone against both others gives the game interesting dynamics.
