Notes for you (not shown to passengers)

  • Starting a Jam: in Spotify, open Now Playing → tap the devices icon (bottom left) → Start a Jam. Your phone then shows the QR code; passengers nearby with Bluetooth on get a Join prompt. That's probably what happened with your passenger.
  • You need Premium to host. Guests can join in person on free Spotify. Joining remotely (not in the car) needs Premium.
  • What guests can do: play, pause, skip, add songs, and reorder the queue. Only you control the volume. You can remove a guest under View Guests, and ending the Jam kicks everyone out.
  • Translations: ES / PT / FR versions get written once you approve the English wording.
